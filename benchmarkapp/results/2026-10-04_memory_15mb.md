# Memory, 15 MB of JSON — macOS arm64, OrcaSecureStorage 2.1.7

Release build, Apple Silicon Mac, [`lib/memory_main.dart`](../lib/memory_main.dart):
46,398 records of ~330 bytes (14.8 MB of JSON). Each storage and phase ran in
a fresh process. Figures are the resident memory the storage added: held two
seconds after the phase, and at its highest.

| Storage | Write: held | Write: peak | Open: held | Open: peak | Write ms | Open + read ms |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| OrcaSecureStorage (password) | 131 MB | 139 MB | 279 MB | 279 MB | 423 | 384 |
| OrcaSecureStorage (key) | 159 MB | 159 MB | 278 MB | 278 MB | 326 | 281 |
| OrcaSecureStorage (no password) | 126 MB | 129 MB | 276 MB | 276 MB | 296 | 282 |
| Hive CE | 98 MB | 98 MB | 160 MB | 160 MB | 1289 | 215 |
| Hive CE (encrypted) | 95 MB | 102 MB | 162 MB | 162 MB | 1679 | 463 |
| Sembast | 82 MB | 82 MB | 121 MB | 121 MB | 4265 | 247 |
| sqflite | 21 MB | 28 MB | 13 MB | 13 MB | 16054 | 2135 |
| SharedPreferences | – | – | – | – | > 4 min (stopped) | – |

GetSecureStorage and GetStorage were not run (they do not finish 10,000
writes within 20 s in the timing benchmark).

Opening is where Orca uses the most memory: its worker decodes the whole
file into objects and sends them to the UI isolate, which builds a second
full copy, before the worker turns its copy into JSON text.
